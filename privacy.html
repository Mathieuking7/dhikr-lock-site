Privacy Policy

Dhikr Lock · last updated 27 August 2026

The short version. Dhikr Lock has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Your adhkar, your streak, your favourites and your reminder times never leave your phone. The one exception is your subscription, which is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, and is described in full below.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is stored locally, in an app group container shared between Dhikr Lock and its widgets. That includes:

  • your progress through a session, and which set you are on;
  • your streak and the days you completed;
  • your favourites;
  • your reminder times and whether reminders are on;
  • the name you optionally entered during onboarding.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. There is no server behind this app for that data, and no way for us to read it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is made through Apple. We never see your payment details, your card, or your Apple Account.

To know whether your subscription is active — including on a second device, or after you reinstall — the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated identifier for your installation, which is not linked to your name, your email or your Apple Account;
  • the purchase receipt Apple issues, and your subscription status;
  • basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version and country, which Apple attaches to the transaction.

That is the only information that leaves your device, it exists solely to answer the question “is this subscription active”, and it is never used for advertising.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your own device. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent from a server, and we do not know whether you opened one.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. There is none to sell.
  • No account, so nothing to leak.

Children

Dhikr Lock is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Everything the app knows about you is on your phone and under your control: delete the app and it is gone. For the subscription record held by RevenueCat, or to ask a question, write to reskype.contact@gmail.com and we will act on it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your device will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
