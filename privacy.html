Privacy Policy

Sanad · last updated 30 August 2026

The short version. Sanad has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no active analytics. Your adhkar, streak, favourites and reminder times stay on your device. Apple and RevenueCat process the limited information needed to manage subscriptions. The TikTok App Events SDK is linked for a future advertising-attribution setup, but it has no valid credentials and is never initialized in this release. It sends no data while disabled. Details are below.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is stored locally, in an app group container shared between Sanad and its widgets. That includes:

  • your progress through a session, and which set you are on;
  • your streak and the days you completed;
  • your favourites;
  • your reminder times and whether reminders are on;
  • the name you optionally entered during onboarding.

None of this practice data is transmitted anywhere. There is no Sanad server for that data and no way for us to read it. You can remove it from your device by deleting the app.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is made through Apple. We never see your payment details, your card, or your Apple Account.

To know whether your subscription is active — including on a second device, or after you reinstall — the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated app user identifier, which Sanad does not link to your name, email or Apple Account;
  • transaction and entitlement information supplied through Apple, such as the product, purchase date, renewal state and subscription status;
  • limited app, device and network information needed to provide and protect the service, such as app version, device type, operating-system version, IP address and storefront or country information.

RevenueCat processes this information to validate purchases, keep entitlements in sync, prevent fraud and provide subscription support. Sanad does not use it for advertising.

Advertising attribution

The app binary includes TikTok's App Events SDK so a future version can measure whether TikTok advertising leads to an install or a subscription. The app requires a complete set of valid TikTok Business configuration values before it can initialize that SDK. Those values are not configured in this release, so the SDK remains disabled. Sanad sends TikTok no app events, advertising identifier, practice data or purchase data while it is disabled.

Before any attribution is activated, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated. Where Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission is required, Sanad will ask first and a refusal will never restrict the app. Your adhkar text, favourites, streak, session progress and reminder times will never be shared with TikTok.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your own device. They are not push notifications, nothing is sent from a server, and we do not know whether you opened one.

What we do not do

  • No advertising is displayed in the app.
  • No advertising attribution or analytics is active in this release.
  • No advertising identifier is accessed or transmitted while TikTok attribution is disabled.
  • Sanad does not sell personal information.
  • Sanad does not create an account or advertising profile about you.

Children

Sanad is not directed primarily to children. The app does not ask for a date of birth and Sanad does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Apple and RevenueCat may process subscription information when a purchase is made. The disabled TikTok attribution integration is not initialized for any user in this release.

Your rights

Sanad does not operate an account or practice-data database. Your adhkar activity can be removed by deleting the app. Apple controls your App Store purchase record. For a request concerning subscription data processed through RevenueCat, or for any privacy question, write to reskype.contact@gmail.com. We may need your anonymous app user identifier to locate a RevenueCat record and will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what leaves your device will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
