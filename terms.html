What the app is

Dhikr Lock presents the morning and evening adhkar with their Arabic text, transliteration, English translation and a reference for each. It lets you count a session, including from your Lock Screen, and keeps a record of the days you completed.

What is free, and what is paid

Free, permanently: every dhikr with its source and grade, both sets in full and without limit, all widgets, the daily reminders, the streak, favourites, search and the per-dhikr counter.

Dhikr Lock Premium unlocks one thing: counting your session from the Lock Screen, without unlocking your phone.

About the content

The adhkar are drawn from the Qur'an and from the recognised hadith collections, and each carries its reference and its grade. Where a long passage is divided into phrases, that division is an editorial judgement about where a reciter naturally pauses; it is a reading aid and carries no ruling. The app is a tool for remembrance, not a source of religious rulings, and it does not replace a qualified scholar. If you find an error in a text, a translation or an attribution, please write to us — corrections are the one kind of message we most want to receive.
