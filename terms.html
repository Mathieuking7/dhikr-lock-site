What the app is

Sanad presents the morning and evening adhkar with their Arabic text, transliteration, English translation and a reference for each. It lets you count a session, including from your Lock Screen, and keeps a record of the days you completed.

What is free, and what is paid

Free: one complete guided morning or evening session per local day, three essential library adhkar, Today's Remembrance and your current streak. The available texts include their Arabic, meaning, transliteration, source and, where available, authenticity grade.

Sanad Premium: unlimited morning and evening sessions, the complete searchable library, individual counters, unlimited favourites, separate morning and evening reminders, seven-day practice history, sharing for Today's Remembrance, all Home and Lock Screen widgets, and interactive session counting from the iPhone Lock Screen through a Live Activity.

About the content

The adhkar are drawn from the Qur'an and from the recognised hadith collections, and each carries its reference and its grade. Where a long passage is divided into phrases, that division is an editorial judgement about where a reciter naturally pauses; it is a reading aid and carries no ruling. The app is a tool for remembrance, not a source of religious rulings, and it does not replace a qualified scholar. If you find an error in a text, a translation or an attribution, please write to us — corrections are the one kind of message we most want to receive.
